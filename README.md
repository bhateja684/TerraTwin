# TerraTwin

## Predictive Digital Twins for Rural and Urban Ecosystems

### Overview

TerraTwin is a research-driven initiative focused on developing predictive digital twins of rural ecosystems using remote sensing, machine learning (Generative Adversarial Networks(GANs)) and generative modelling. Rather than merely observing environmental conditions, TerraTwin aims to understand, simulate and forecast how ecosystems evolve under natural processes and human interventions.

The central question motivating TerraTwin is:

**Can we build computational representations of ecosystems that not only describe the present but also reason about possible futures?**

By integrating Earth observation data, environmental indicators and machine learning models, TerraTwin seeks to create dynamic digital representations capable of modelling ecosystem behaviour under varying conditions and intervention scenarios.


## Research Motivation

Natural systems are increasingly influenced by climate variability, resource pressures and human decision-making. While modern remote sensing provides unprecedented visibility into environmental change, most existing approaches remain descriptive rather than predictive.

TerraTwin explores a shift from observation to simulation.

The project investigates how machine learning systems can learn spatiotemporal patterns from satellite imagery and environmental signals to model ecosystem evolution, quantify uncertainty and forecast future states under alternative scenarios.

At its core, TerraTwin is an effort to bridge remote sensing, scientific machine learning and environmental intelligence.


## Research Themes

* Spatiotemporal Representation Learning
* Ecosystem Forecasting
* Scientific Machine Learning
* Environmental Digital Twins
* Remote Sensing and Earth Observation
* Generative Modelling for Future-State Simulation
* Uncertainty-Aware Machine Learning
* Scenario-Based Environmental Analysis


## Long-Term Vision

TerraTwin aims to evolve into a platform capable of answering questions such as:

* How will an ecosystem evolve over the next 5, 10 or 20 years?
* What are the projected consequences of specific interventions?
* Which environmental variables contribute most significantly to observed change?
* How can uncertainty in environmental forecasts be quantified and communicated?

The long-term objective is to develop a scalable framework for environmental intelligence that supports researchers, policymakers and communities in understanding the future behaviour of complex ecosystems.



## Current Status

TerraTwin is currently under active research and development, focusing on the integration of satellite imagery, spatiotemporal learning methods, ecosystem representation frameworks and predictive modelling approaches for future-state simulation.
